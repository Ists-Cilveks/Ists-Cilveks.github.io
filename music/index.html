What?

This is an experiment of key patterns with a ratio of φ. The music is made of long and short beats according to the Fibonacci word: long short long, long short, long short long. long short long, long short. etc. You can then think of the music as having long and short measures that follow that same pattern.

This idea came about while trying to come up with a number system that doesn't use natural numbers.
